How we work

Our principles

Problem first, tool second
We never recommend automation for the sake of it. Every system we build targets a specific, measurable business pain — and we define success criteria before writing a single node.
Modular by design
Every agent works independently and can be added or removed without breaking the system. Start small, scale when ready — no expensive rebuilds.
Production-ready, not demo-ready
Every delivery includes error handling, logging, monitoring, and documentation. If something breaks at 2am, the system alerts you — and you know exactly how to fix it.
You own it completely
Every project is delivered with full documentation, SOPs, and training. Your team can operate the system independently — no lock-in, no dependency on us to keep the lights on.
Direct access, always
You work directly with the founder — not a project manager, not a junior developer. The person who scoped your project is the person who builds it.
Transparent pricing
No hidden fees, no surprise invoices. We publish our pricing ranges openly and provide a detailed cost breakdown — including third-party tool costs — before any project starts.
